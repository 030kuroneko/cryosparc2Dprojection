# Separate camera matching from surface rendering

Determine every Class Camera Orientation only from the unsharpened Matching Map, then treat the Rendering Map, Surface Level, surface styling, and oblique inspection tilt as presentation choices. This boundary preserves the scientific camera result when users switch to a sharpened map or adjust the surface for clearer inspection, while retaining an exact orthographic Camera View Render beside a deliberately non-exact Oblique Inspection Render.
